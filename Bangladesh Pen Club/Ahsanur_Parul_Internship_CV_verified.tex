\projectheading
{Bangladeshi Fish Species Classification}
{}
{Python, PyTorch, scikit-learn, OpenCV, XGBoost, VGG19, ResNet50, EfficientNet-B3}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Fine-tuned VGG19, ResNet50, and EfficientNet-B3 on \textbf{26,950 images across 20 fish species}; ResNet50 achieved \textbf{99.9\% test accuracy} using a stratified train/validation/test split and augmentation restricted to the training set.
    \item Designed custom CNN variants with \textbf{SE, CBAM, and coordinate attention}; CBAM achieved \textbf{98.9\% accuracy}, while an SVM on learned features reached \textbf{99.1\%}. Evaluated with confusion matrices, ROC curves, Grad-CAM, and inference latency (2.5--4.3 ms/image).
\end{itemize}
