About the club

Bangladesh Pen Club is a community for anyone who still reaches for a pen. Some of us collect vintage fountain pens. Some practise Bangla and English calligraphy. Some write letters to friends in other districts and other countries.

  • Collectors swap pens, share restoration tips and compare inks.
  • Calligraphers run practice sessions for all skill levels.
  • Pen pals are matched with a writer in another city.
